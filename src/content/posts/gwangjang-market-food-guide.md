**Gwangjang Market** opened over a hundred years ago and is often called Korea's first permanent market. It was famous for fabric, but today most visitors come for the food alley in the middle — rows of stalls frying, chopping and steaming under strings of flags.

## What to eat

- **Bindaetteok (빈대떡)** — a thick mung bean pancake, crispy at the edges. Many stalls sell it with meatballs as a set.
- **Makgeolli (막걸리)** — milky rice wine, the classic match for pancakes.
- **Mayak kimbap (마약김밥)** — mini rice rolls dipped in mustard soy sauce, easy to eat while walking.
- **Yukhoe (육회)** — Korean beef tartare with egg yolk. The famous shops have long lines, so go early.

## How to get there

Jongno 5-ga Station (Line 1), Exit 8, or Euljiro 4-ga Station (Lines 2 & 5), Exit 4. The food stalls are just inside the east and south gates.

## Tips

- Prices are on the menu boards — check before you order.
- Share a few plates between friends so you can try more.
- In December, combine dinner here with the [Seoul Lantern Festival](/posts/seoul-lantern-festival-2026/) along Cheonggyecheon.
