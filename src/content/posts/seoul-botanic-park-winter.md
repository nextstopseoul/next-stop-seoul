When Seoul gets cold, **Seoul Botanic Park** in Magok is one of the easiest places to feel warm again. Its bowl-shaped glass greenhouse holds a tropical forest and a Mediterranean garden — and this winter, a special exhibition of orchids and poinsettias.

## This winter's exhibition

From November 13, 2026 to January 31, 2027, the greenhouse hosts a winter show with the National Institute of Horticultural and Herbal Science. The tropical zone focuses on orchids, with around 40 species, while the Mediterranean zone becomes a Christmas garden of Korean-bred poinsettias.

The Seed Library next door is free and hosts a seeds-and-fruits exhibition from December 1.

## How to get there

Take Line 9 or the Airport Railroad (AREX) to **Magongnaru Station** and walk to the park. It's handy if you're flying from Gimpo Airport — Magongnaru is a few minutes away by train.

## Tips

- Check opening days on the official site before you go.
- Bring a layer you can take off — the tropical zone is warm and humid.
- Pair it with a walk around the outdoor lake garden on a clear day.
